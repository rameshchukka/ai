Shipping Policy

Standard shipping within the continental United States takes 5-7 business days and
is free on orders over $50. Orders under $50 incur a flat $6.99 shipping fee.

Express shipping (1-2 business days) is available for an additional $12 on domestic
orders only. Express shipping is NOT available for international orders under any
circumstances.

International orders (outside the United States) ship via standard international
freight and may take up to 21 business days depending on customs processing in the
destination country. International orders over $150 qualify for free standard
international shipping; orders under $150 incur a $24.99 international shipping fee.

Orders cannot be redirected to a different address once shipped. Address changes must
be requested within 1 hour of order placement.
