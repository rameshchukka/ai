Escalation Policy

Support tickets should be escalated to a Tier 2 specialist if any of the following
apply: the customer has contacted support 3 or more times about the same issue, the
issue involves a potential data loss or security concern, or the customer explicitly
requests to speak with a manager.

Tier 2 specialists have authority to approve refund exceptions outside the standard
policy window, up to $200 in value, without further approval. Exceptions above $200
require sign-off from a Support Team Lead.

Any ticket mentioning legal action, regulatory complaints, or media/press inquiries
must be escalated immediately to the Legal & Compliance team and should NOT be
handled directly by support staff, regardless of ticket urgency classification.
