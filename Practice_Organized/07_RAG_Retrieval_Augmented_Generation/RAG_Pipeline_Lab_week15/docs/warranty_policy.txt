Warranty Policy

All hardware products include a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing
defects and workmanship issues. This warranty does NOT cover accidental damage,
water damage, or damage caused by unauthorized modification.

Extended warranties (covering years 2 and 3) can be purchased within 30 days of the
original purchase date, at a cost of $49/year. Extended warranties cannot be added
after the initial 30-day window has passed, even if the standard 1-year warranty is
still active.

To file a warranty claim, the customer must provide the original order number and a
description of the defect. Approved claims are resolved via free repair or full
replacement, at the company's discretion. Warranty claims do not cover shipping costs
for returning the defective item, but return shipping for the replacement is free.
