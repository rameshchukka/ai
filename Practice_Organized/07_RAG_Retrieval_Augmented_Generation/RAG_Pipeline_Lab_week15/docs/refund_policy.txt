Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 14 days of the original purchase date,
provided the item is unopened and in its original packaging. After 14 days but within
30 days, only store credit is issued, and only for unopened items. Digital goods
(software licenses, downloadable content) are non-refundable once the download has
been initiated, regardless of how much time has passed.

Refunds for annual subscription plans are prorated based on unused months, but only
if requested within the first 30 days of the subscription term. After 30 days, annual
subscriptions are non-refundable but can be downgraded to a lower tier at the next
renewal date.

Damaged or defective items are eligible for a full refund or replacement at any time
within the 1-year warranty period (see Warranty Policy), regardless of the standard
14-day refund window.
