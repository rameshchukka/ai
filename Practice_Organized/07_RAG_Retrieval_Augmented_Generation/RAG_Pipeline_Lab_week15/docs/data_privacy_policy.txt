Data Privacy Policy

Customer data is retained for the duration of an active account, plus 90 days after
account closure, after which it is permanently deleted unless required to be retained
longer for legal or tax purposes.

Customers may request a full export of their data at any time via the account
settings page; exports are generated within 48 hours and made available for download
for 7 days.

Data deletion requests ("right to be forgotten") are processed within 30 days and
cannot be reversed once completed. Support staff cannot process data deletion
requests directly - these must be submitted through the official data request form
and are handled by the Data Privacy team, not general support.
