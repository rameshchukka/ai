Billing Policy

Monthly subscriptions are billed on the same calendar day each month as the original
signup date. If a customer signs up on the 31st of a month, billing occurs on the
last day of shorter months.

Failed payments trigger 3 retry attempts over 7 days. If all retries fail, the
account is downgraded to a free/limited tier but is not deleted; account data is
retained for 90 days before permanent deletion.

Plan upgrades take effect immediately and are billed as a prorated charge for the
remainder of the current billing cycle. Plan downgrades take effect at the start of
the next billing cycle; no prorated refund is issued for the difference.

Duplicate charges due to a processing error are automatically flagged by the billing
system within 24 hours and refunded without requiring a customer support ticket. If a
customer reports a duplicate charge that has not yet been auto-flagged, support should
verify via the billing dashboard before promising a refund.
