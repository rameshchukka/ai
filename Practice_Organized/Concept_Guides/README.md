# 📖 Concept Guides — Read Before You Practice

Nine short, reading-first PDF guides covering every math/algorithm-heavy topic in the course.
No code, no exercises — just the concept, the formula (built up term by term), a fully worked
numeric example by hand, the intuition in plain language, and common misunderstandings. Each
guide ends with a pointer to the matching hands-on notebook chapter for practice.

**Why a separate format from the notebooks:** the notebooks are optimized for *doing* (run
code, see output, exercise, solution). These guides are optimized for *understanding first* —
the kind of material you sit and think through, work out on paper, maybe read on your phone or
print out, before touching a keyboard.

## How to Use

1. **Read the concept guide first**, cover to cover — it's short (4-5 pages each), meant for one
   sitting. Work through the "Worked Example" boxes by hand with pen and paper.
2. **Then open the matching notebook** in `../Zero_to_Hero_Series/` and do the hands-on
   exercises — you'll recognize every formula from the guide, now applied in running code.
3. Keep the guide open as a reference while coding — the "Common Misunderstandings" boxes are
   worth re-reading when you hit a confusing bug.

## What Each Guide Contains

- **The concept in plain English** — why this idea exists, what problem it solves
- **The formula**, introduced piece by piece, never dropped whole without explanation
- **A worked numeric example** — real numbers, by hand, so you can check your own understanding
- **A mental model** — one memorable way to think about the idea
- **Common misunderstandings** — the mistakes that trip people up most often
- **A pointer to the matching notebook** for hands-on practice

## Modules Covered by Guide vs. Lighter on Math

The remaining Zero-to-Hero modules (Pandas, LLM APIs, RAG, LangChain, Agents, MCP, A2A,
Evaluation & Guardrails, MLOps) are architecture/pattern-focused rather than math-heavy, so they
don't have a dedicated concept guide — their notebooks' built-in theory sections (📖 Theory boxes
at the start of each chapter) serve the same purpose for those topics.
