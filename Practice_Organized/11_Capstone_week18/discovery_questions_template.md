## Test for each question before finalizing it
- Could a non-technical person actually answer this? (If it uses words like "API,"
  "latency," or "embedding," rewrite it.)
- If they answered the OPPOSITE of what you expect, would your architecture actually
  change? (If not, it's not a real discovery question — it's a confirmation question.)
