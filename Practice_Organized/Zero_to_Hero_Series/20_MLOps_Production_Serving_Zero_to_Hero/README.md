# 🏭 MLOps & Production Serving: Zero to Hero — Guided Lab

The operational discipline that keeps a model reliable after the notebook work is done: serving patterns, latency/throughput budgets, caching, monitoring, data/model drift detection, A/B testing and canary rollouts, rich LLM observability, and cost management at scale. Capstone verified: a working monitored, cached, guarded serving layer.

## The teaching format (every chapter)
- 📖 **Theory** (detailed) — the concept explained properly, not just name-dropped
- 🧠 **Mental model** — the intuition to hold in your head
- 🖼️ **ASCII diagram** — a visual of how it fits together
- 🔬 **Worked example** — runnable code you execute and read
- ⚡ **Pro tips** and ⚠️ **Common traps** — what actually trips people up
- ✏️ **Your Turn** exercise → ✅ **Solution** (revealed right after)
