📘 Concept Notes

RNN information loss is measurable, not just claimed

Feeding a distinct signal into an RNN's hidden state, then 30 steps of unrelated input, drops the hidden state's similarity to its original post-signal value to near zero. This is the concrete mechanism behind 'RNNs struggle with long-range dependencies' -- not a metaphor.

The whole architecture's shape story is one sentence

Embeddings enter a Transformer block as (seq_len, d_model) and leave as (seq_len, d_model) -- identical shape. The only place the shape changes is the intermediate attention-weights matrix, (seq_len, seq_len). This is exactly why blocks can stack indefinitely.

Training and inference run the SAME architecture differently

Training uses teacher forcing: the full target sequence is fed in at once and every position's prediction is computed in one parallel matrix operation. Inference is inherently sequential -- each new token depends on the last -- which is why KV-caching (reusing already-computed Keys/Values) is a mandatory production optimization, not a nicety.

Trust your understanding by matching a real framework exactly

Building multi-head attention from scratch and confirming it matches torch.nn.MultiheadAttention's output to floating-point precision, given the same weights, is proof the mental model is mechanically correct -- not an approximation of what production frameworks do.

💡 Tips & Pitfalls

Tips

  • Render every attention pattern you compute -- the picture is where the understanding actually happens.
  • Compare against a real framework whenever you can, not just against your own expectations.
  • Distance doesn't weaken attention the way it weakens an RNN's hidden state -- content similarity does the driving.

Common Pitfalls

  • Assuming more attention heads is always better (each head needs enough capacity to be useful).
  • Confusing training-time parallelism with inference-time parallelism -- inference is inherently sequential.
  • Explaining backprop/attention in the vocabulary of one specific implementation instead of the general concept.

🌍 Real-World Use Cases

  • Understanding what production LLM serving stacks (vLLM, TGI) optimize and why
  • Debugging 'why is generation slow' vs 'why is training slow' as genuinely different questions
