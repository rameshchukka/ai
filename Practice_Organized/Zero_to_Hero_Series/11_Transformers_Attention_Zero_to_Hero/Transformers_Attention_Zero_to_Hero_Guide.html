📘 Concept Notes

Attention replaces a shrinking memory with direct access

Older sequence models (RNNs) squeeze the whole sentence through one fixed-size hidden state, losing early information over long sequences. Attention lets every word directly look at every other word at once, so nothing has to survive a long relay race.

Query, Key, Value is a search metaphor

Query is what a word is looking for, Key is what every word advertises about itself, and Value is the actual content offered. Comparing a word's Query against every Key produces attention scores that weight how much of each Value to gather.

The attention formula in one line

softmax(QK^T / sqrt(d_k)) V: dot-product similarity between queries and keys, scaled to keep softmax stable, turned into weights that sum to 1, then used to compute a weighted average of values.

Causal masking is what makes GPT generate left-to-right

Setting future positions' attention scores to -infinity before softmax (so they become 0) stops a generating model from cheating by looking at words it hasn't produced yet. This is the core difference between GPT-style and BERT-style models.

💡 Tips & Pitfalls

Tips

  • Say the shapes out loud at each step — attention is 90% shape bookkeeping.
  • Residual connections aren't polish — they're what makes deep stacks trainable.
  • BERT and GPT differ mainly by presence/absence of the causal mask.

Common Pitfalls

  • Forgetting positional encoding (attention alone can't tell word order).
  • Skipping the /sqrt(d_k) scale factor.
  • Missing the causal mask when building a text generator (lets it see the future).
